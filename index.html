House Rules & Stay Policy

A few clear guidelines help us keep every stay safe, peaceful and comfortable for all guests.

01

Identity & Guest Charges

Valid government photo ID is mandatory at check-in. Extra guests are charged ₹1,500 per person, per day. Children above 5 years are chargeable.

02

Quiet Hours & Checkout

Outdoor music is strictly not allowed after 9:00 PM. Late checkout is subject to availability and charged at ₹2,000 per hour.

03

Pool Safety

The pool is strictly open from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM and is 4 ft deep. Food and drinks are not allowed in or around the pool. Diving and jumping are strictly prohibited. Children must be supervised by an adult. Guests use the pool at their own risk.

04

Power Backup

Village-wide electricity outages are beyond the property's control. Inverter backup is available for approximately 4 hours during an outage.

05

Cancellation Policy

Advance payments are non-refundable in case of natural calamities, road blockages, landslides or political activity affecting travel.

06

Care for the Villa

The refundable deposit is returned after checkout inspection, subject to no damage or loss. Any damage caused to the property, furniture, or amenities will be charged extra to the guest on top of the deposit. Please follow caretaker guidance and use all facilities responsibly.

Nearby Attractions & Distances

Meraki Hillside Villa is conveniently located close to Panchgani's most iconic viewpoints, gardens, and markets.

01
7 MINS DRIVE

Sydney Point

Famous hill view spot offering 360° panoramic views of Dhom Dam, Krishna Valley, and surrounding green hills.

02
10 MINS DRIVE

Table Land Plateau

Asia's second-longest mountain plateau. Perfect for evening horse riding, photography, and sunset strolls.

03
12 MINS DRIVE

Mapro Garden

Must-visit culinary spot famous for fresh Panchgani strawberry cream, wood-fired pizzas, and regional fruit preserves.

04
15 MINS DRIVE

Parsi Point

Scenic viewpoint overlooking the vast expanse of Dhom Dam backwaters and lush valley slopes.

05
20 MINS DRIVE

Dhom Dam Boating

Enjoy serene water sports, speed boating, and quiet lakeside picnics along the pristine reservoir shores.

Panchgani Town Market

Explore vibrant local shops for fresh chikki, chana, homemade fruit syrups, and handcrafted leather items.
