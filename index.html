RECOMMENDED STAY GUIDE

Your Perfect 2-Day Weekend Itinerary in Panchgani

Here is how guests experience a memorable weekend retreat at Meraki.

DAY 1 • ARRIVAL & SUNSET VIBES
  • 2:00 PM – Check-In & Welcome Drinks

    Arrive at Meraki Hillside Villa, sip on fresh welcome kokum sherbet, and settle into your luxury suites.

  • 4:30 PM – Infinity Pool & Valley Views

    Take a long, relaxing swim in the cliffside pool as the sun dips over the Panchgani dam and valley.

  • 7:30 PM – Live Charcoal BBQ & Fairy Light Garden

    Gather in the lawn under fairy lights for grilled tikkas, snacks, and music around a warm bonfire.

DAY 2 • EXPLORE & UNWIND
  • 9:00 AM – Fresh Cooked Breakfast on Deck

    Enjoy hot Poha, Misal Pav, Parathas, and fresh Panchgani strawberries with morning chai.

  • 11:30 AM – Local Viewpoints & Strawberry Tasting

    Visit Sydney Point (7 mins) & Mapro Garden (12 mins) for fresh strawberry cream and jams.

  • 8:00 PM – Traditional Feast & Games Night

    Enjoy authentic Maharashtrian chicken/paneer curry by our cook, followed by table tennis and carrom.
